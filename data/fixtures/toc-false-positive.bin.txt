toc-false-positive.bin -- 2,048 bytes excerpted from the CONCRETE texture lump
of freedoom1.wad (Freedoom 0.13.0), kept as a regression specimen for
core/forensics/toc.py.

It is here because it fooled the table-of-contents detector into reporting a
100-entry table at confidence 0.84. Image data sits in a narrow band of byte
values, that band overlaps printable ASCII, and byte 0x5C is an ordinary pixel,
so a run of pixels satisfied "looks like a Windows path". Synthetic data does
not reproduce this; the real bytes do.
